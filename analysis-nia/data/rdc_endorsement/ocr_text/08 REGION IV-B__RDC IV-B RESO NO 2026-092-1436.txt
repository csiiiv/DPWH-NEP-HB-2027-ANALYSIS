RDC Resolution No. _2026-092-1436

FAVORABLY ENDORSING THE FISCAL YEAR (FY) 2027 BUDGET PROPOSAL OF
THE NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION (NIA) MIMAROPA

WHEREAS, Section 4 of Executive Order (EO) 82, s. 2025 mandates the
Regional Development Councils (RDCs) to review and endorse the annual
budgets of regional offices, including national and local government units’
(LGUs) projects that support the Regional Development Plan (RDP), that
require national government funding, and have significant regional impacts;

WHEREAS, Section 5 of EO 82 directs the Department of Economy, Planning,
and Development (DEPDev) and Department of Budget and Management
(DBM) to jointly issue guidelines to strengthen regional investment
programming and budgeting, including the prioritization of key programs and
projects (PAPs) in the RDPs;

WHEREAS, the Regional-National Investment Programming (RNIP) Dialogue
was institutionalized to align regional priorities with national investment
programming, through which 28 Agency Regional-National Investment
Projects (ARNIPs) for FY 2027 were identified as regional priority projects of
the MIMAROPA RDC and select agencies’;

WHEREAS, on 05 January 2026, the DBM issued National Budget
Memorandum (NBM) No. 156 or the National Budget Call for FY 2027,
providing the guidelines for the preparation of the FY 2027 budget proposals
and directing agency regional offices (AROs) to prepare budget proposals
consistent with national priorities and subject to RDC review;

WHEREAS, on 12 January 2026, the DEPDev and DBM MIMAROPA Regional
Offices issued a Joint Memorandum providing the regional guidelines for the
preparation, review, and endorsement of the FY 2027 budget proposals of
AROs and SUCs;

WHEREAS, the National Budget Call prescribes the continued adoption of the
two-tier budgeting approach, with Tier 1 covering ongoing PAPs and Tier 2
covering new or expanded but implementation-ready PAPs;

WHEREAS, NIA MIMAROPA proposed two hundred eighteen (218) Tier 1 PAPs
amounting to Php5.64 billion, one (1) of which is an ARNIP (Annex A);

WHEREAS, NIA MIMAROPA certifies that all Tier 1 PAPs in their FY 2027
budget proposal are currently ongoing and duly existing;

WHEREAS, NIA MIMAROPA also proposed seventy (70) Tier 2 projects
amounting to Php1.08 billion, two (2) of which are ARNIPs (Annex B);

‘NIP Dialogue Pilot Agency Central Offices (ACOS), i.e., DA, DepEd, DENR, DHSUD, DICT, DILG, DOE, DOH, DOST, DOTr, DSWD, NIA, and DPWH.

WHEREAS, NIA MIMAROPA certifies that its Tier 2 projects in its FY 2027
budget proposal are implementation-ready and have fully complied with all
required documents, permits, approvals, and other requirements under
existing laws and government regulations;

WHEREAS, the proposed PAPs of NIA MIMAROPA were found consistent with
the updated MIMAROPA Regional Development Investment Program (RDIP)
2023-2028 and supportive of the MIMAROPA Regional Development Plan
2023-2028 Midterm Update;

NOW, THEREFORE, ON A MOTION DULY SECONDED, be it

RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, That the Council favorably
endorses the FY 2027 budget proposal of the NIA MIMAROPA;

RESOLVED FURTHER, That the proposed regional programs and projects for
FY 2027, including the identified ARNIPs, be included in the FY 2027 budget
proposal of the NIA Central Office and in the MIMAROPA RDIP 2023-2028;

RESOLVED FURTHER, That the BP Form C containing Summary of RDC
Inputs and Recommendations, as prescribed in the NBM No. 156, be submitted
by NIA to the RDC to ensure timely feedback and effective budget monitoring;

RESOLVED FURTHER, That a copy of this Resolution be provided to the NIA
Central Office for appropriate action; and

RESOLVED FINALLY, That copies of this Resolution be furnished to the DBM
and the MIMAROPA Congressional District Representatives for their
information and appropriate budgetary and legislative support;

UNANIMOUSLY APPROVED.
