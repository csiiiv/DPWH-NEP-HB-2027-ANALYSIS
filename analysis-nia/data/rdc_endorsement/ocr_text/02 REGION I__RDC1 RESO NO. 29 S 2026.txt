RDC-1 Full Council Resolution No. 29, Series 2026

ENDORSING TO THE HONORABLE EDUARDO EDDIE G. GUILLEN,
ADMINISTRATOR OF THE NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION, THE FY 2027
PROPOSED BUDGET FOR PRIORITY PROGRAMS AND PROJECTS OF THE
NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION REGIONAL OFFICE 1, FOR INCLUSION
IN THE FINAL FY 2027 AGENCY BUDGET PROPOSAL

WHEREAS, pursuant to Executive Order No. 82, $. 2025, which reconstitutes and strengthens
the Regional. Development Councils (RDCs) io accelerate development in all regions, and
Memorandum Circular: No. 54, s, 2004, which reinforces the RDCs’ role in the review and
endorsement of the national government budget, the RDCs are mandated to review and
endorse to the national government the annual budgets of agency regional offices (AROs), state
universities and colleges (SUCs), and spécial development authorities to ensure that priority
programs and projects are consistent with and:supportive of the regional thrusts and priorities
as contained in the Regional Development Plan (RDP) and its results matrices, and the Regional
Development Investment Program;

WHEREAS, National Budget Memorandum No. 156 dated January 5, 2026, provides for the
policy guidelines and procedures in the preparation of the FY 2027 Budget Proposals and calls
for the conduct of consultations with the RDCs, and with other concerned stakeholders to
strengthen the vertical linkage between national and regional/local government development
plans and budgets;

WHEREAS, RDC-1 issued the Regional Budgeting Guidelines on February 13, 2026, to aid in
the preparation, review and endorsement of the priority programs and projects. of AROs, SUCs
and Government-owned and Controlled Corporations (GOCCs) for inclusion in the FY
2027 Agency Budget Proposals;

WHEREAS, the Regional Development Budget Coordination Committee-1 (RDBCC-1) reviewed
the FY 2027 proposed budgets for priority programs and projects during the FY 2027 Regional
Budget Review and Consultation held last 4-6 March 2026;

WHEREAS, the identified priority programs and projects of the National Irrigation Administration
Regional Office 1 (NIA-RO1) are consistent with the requirements of NBM No. 156;

WHEREAS, the identified priority programs and projects of NIA RO1 are supportive of and in
conformity with the regional development thrust and priority of expanding and upgrading digital
and physical infrastructure as contained in the RDP 2023-2028;

WHEREAS, the final FY 2027 Proposed Budget of NIA RO1 integrated the agency’s project
listed under the Key Priority Projects for FY 2027 and likewise considered the comments,
suggestions and recommendations raised during the FY 2027 Regional Budget Review and
Consultation;
