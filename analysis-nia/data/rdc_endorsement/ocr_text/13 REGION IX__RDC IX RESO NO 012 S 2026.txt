WHEREAS, the Regional Development Council (RDC) IX is mandated to coordinate,
integrate, and monitor development initiatives in the region, including the
review and endorsement of agency priority programs, activities, and projects
(P/A/Ps) for funding consideration under their respective Regional
Development Investment Programs (RDIPs) and annual budget proposals;

WHEREAS, | the National Budget Memorandum (NBM) No. 156 issued by the Department
of Budget and Management (DBM) prescribes the guidelines and procedures
for the preparation of agency budget proposals for Fiscal Year (FY) 2027,
including the timelines, required forms, and mechanisms to ensure alignment
of agency proposals with national and regional development priorities;

WHEREAS, §asalient feature of NBM No. 156 is the strengthened linkage between national
budgeting and regional development planning, requiring agencies to duly
consider and reflect RDC-endorsed PAPs in their budget proposals and to
provide feedback on the action taken thereon;

WHEREAS, pursuant to NBM No. 156, Budget Preparation (BP) Form C is prescribed to
capture the inputs and recommendations of the RDCs on agency P/A/Ps and
shall be prepared by the Agency Central Offices (ACOs), in coordination with
their respective Agency Regional Offices (AROs), as well as by State
Universities and Colleges (SUCs);

WHEREAS, BP Form C reinforces the ACO-RDC feedback mechanism, whereby ACOs,
through their AROs, and SUCs shall provide the concerned RDCs with
feedback on the disposition of RDC-endorsed PAPs, clearly indicating which
items were included, partially included, or not included in their budget
proposals, together with the corresponding justifications;

WHEREAS, | the submission of the duly accomplished BP Form C to RDC IX will enable
the Council to effectively monitor the responsiveness of the national budget to
regional development priorities and strengthen transparency, accountability,
and evidence-based planning and programming in the region;
