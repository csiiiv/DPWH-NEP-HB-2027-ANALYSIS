WHEREAS, based on the National Budget Memorandum (NBM) No. 156 or the National
Budget Call for FY 2027 issued by the Department of Budget and Management, the RDC
XI issued the Guidelines for the Preparation, Review and Consultation on the FY 2027
Budget Proposals for Davao Region on January 20, 2026 to guide the preparation, review,
and endorsement of the Agency FY 2027 Budget Proposals (BPs);

WHEREAS, the FY 2027 budget review exercise ensures the linkage across development
planning, investment programming, and budgeting processes, as well as, guarantees the
responsiveness of agency regional offices’ (AROs), state universities and colleges’
(SUCs), and selected Government-Owned and Controlled Corporations’ (GOCCs) budget
proposals to the goals, objectives and targets espoused in the Davao Regional
Development Plan (DRDP), 2023-2028 and its accompanying Result Matrices (RMs);

WHEREAS, this Council's Sectoral Committees served as Reviewing/Endorsing Panels
designated by the RDC XI as specified in the RDC XI Guidelines for the Preparation,
Review and Consultation on FY 2027 Budget Proposals;

WHEREAS, the Acting Regional Manager of the NIA XI, Engr. Dexter A. Sablaon,
presented before the Infrastructure Development Committee XI during its CY 2026 ‘st
Quarter Meeting on February 26, 2026 the Agency’s FY 2027 Budget Proposal, which the
Committee finds to be aligned with and responsive to the outcomes in the DRDP, 2023-
2028 and its Results Matrices, but needs to consider the Committee's comments as
detailed in the BP Form C attached as Annex B;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, that this
Council favorably endorse the NIA XI’s FY 2027 Budget Proposal, viz:
