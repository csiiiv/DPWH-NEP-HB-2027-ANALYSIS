APPROVING THE 2025 MIDTERM UPDATE OF THE WESTERN VISAYAS
REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT INVESTMENT PROGRAM (RDIP) 2023-2028

WHEREAS, Section 4(a) of Executive Order (EO) No. 82, s. 2025, mandates
the Regional Development Councils to lead the preparation, implementation,
monitoring, and evaluation of regional development investment programs (RDIPs);

WHEREAS, Section 36(d) of Republic Act No. 12145, s. 2025 (Economy,
Planning and Development Act), further mandates the RDCs to review and prioritize
programs and projects that support the regional development plan (RDP), as
contained in the RDIP, and endorse to the national government those requiring funding
support;

WHEREAS, RDC VI approved the 2025 Midterm Update of the Western
Visayas RDP 2023-2028 * in September 29, 2025 through Resolution No. 124, s. 2025;

WHEREAS, pursuant to Sections 5(b)(2) and 18 of Republic Act No. 12145
(Economic, Planning, and Development Act), DEPDev Region VI spearheaded and
prepared 2025 Midterm Update of the WV RDIP 2023-2028 in coordination with
agency regional offices (AROs) and state universities (SUs) in the region;

WHEREAS, the 2025 Midterm Update of the WV RDIP 2023-2028 aims to:

= ensure that public investments of AROs and SUs will align with the strategic
goals and priorities of the 2025 Midterm Update of the WV RDP 2023-2028,

= reconfirm priority programs and projects (PAPs) for 2026-2028 and drop
previously submitted PAPs that are no longer priorities; and

= include PAPs from the National Public Investment Program that are on-going
or for implementation in Western Visayas, as well as the PAPs that are duly
endorsed by the RDC VI for local or national government funding;
