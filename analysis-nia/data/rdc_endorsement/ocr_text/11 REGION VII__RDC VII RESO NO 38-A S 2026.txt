Dear Engr. Guillen:

This is to transmit the Central Visayas Regional Development Council (RDC)
Resolution No. 38-A (s. 2026), endorsing the FY 2027 budget proposal of the
National Irrigation Administration Regional Office 7 (NIA 7) to the NIA Central Office
for inclusion in the FY 2027 budget proposal of the agency.

This endorsement is issued in accordance with Section 2.2 of the National Budget
Call for 2027 requiring budget proposals of agencies to undergo RDC review
process and endorsement. Likewise, may we remind the NIA Central Office to
provide the Central Visayas RDC with initial feedback on the inclusion of the PAPs
that were endorsed by the Council in the agency’s budget proposal submitted to the
Department of Budget and Management. Pursuant to Section 2.2.3 of the budget
call, the RDC needs to be informed which of the PAPs were or were not included in
the agency’s budget proposal for FY 2027.

The Central Visayas RDC would appreciate NIA’s consideration of all the endorsed
PAPs in the FY 2027 budget proposal of the agency.

Thank you.

Very truly yours,

GOV. PAMELA S. BARICUATRO

RDC Resolution No. 38-A (8. 2026)

“ENDORSING THE FY 2027 BUDGET PROPOSAL OF THE
NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION REGIONAL OFFICE 7 (NIA 7)
TO THE NIA CENTRAL OFFICE FOR INCLUSION IN THE
FY 2027 BUDGET PROPOSAL OF THE AGENCY”

WHEREAS, Section 2.2.1 of National Budget Memorandum (NBM) No. 156 or
the National Budget Call for FY 2027, issued by the Department of Budget and
Management (DBM) on 5 January 2026, directs agency regional offices (AROs) to
craft annual regional plans and budgets anchored on a set of priorities and criteria
and to coordinate their efforts with their counterpart Regional Development Councils
(RDCs) to ensure alignment of regional programs with that of the agency céntral
offices’ national programs;

WHEREAS, NBM No. 153 directs agency central offices, through their AROs,
as well as State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), to provide timely feedback to the
RDCs, through Budget Preparation Form © (BP form ©), on the items that were or
were not included in the submitted agency budget proposals;

WHEREAS, in preparation for the RDC review and endorsement of the FY 2027
annual budgets of agency regional offices and SUCs, the RDC sent an advisory to
the regional line agencies and SUCs on 19 January 2026 calling for the submission
of duly accomplished RDC-prescribed budget review forms, namely Annex A and
Annex B;

WHEREAS, Annex A contains information on the total budgetary requirements
of the agency/SUC, broken down by object of expenditure and geographic
distribution/scope, while Annex B is DBM’s BP Form C, containing the RDC’s
comments and recommendations on the PAPs for their central offices’ feedback;

WHEREAS, the NIA 7 submitted to the IDC Secretariat the accomplished RDC
budget forms;

WHEREAS, pursuant to the RDC-approved Guidelines for the Formulation of
the Central Visayas RDIP and Annual Investment Program (AIP), the FY 2027 AIP
was used as the basis for recommending RDC endorsement of proposed PAPs for
inclusion in the annual agency budget proposals;

WHEREAS, for new or expanded PAPs already approved for inclusion in the
RDIP before CY 2026, they are additionally assessed for implementation-readiness,
supported by certification from the agency/SUC;

WHEREAS, the RDC Infrastructure Development Committee has recommended
the endorsement of the budgetary requirement of NIA 7 amounting to Nine Billion
Three Hundred Sixty-Five Million Twenty-Eighty Thousand Four Hundred Fifty
Pesos for inclusion in the FY 2027 budget proposal;
