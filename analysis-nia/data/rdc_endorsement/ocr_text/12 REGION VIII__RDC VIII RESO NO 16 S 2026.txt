RDC Vill Resolution No. 16, Series of 2026

REQUESTING THE AGENCY CENTRAL OFFICES (ACOs) TO FURNISH THE DEPARTMENT
OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT (DBM) WITH COPIES OF THE DETAILED SUB-ALLOTMENT
FOR THE REGIONAL LINE AGENCIES (RLAs) IN EASTERN VISAYAS

WHEREAS, Section 5 of Executive Order No. 82, Series of 2025, directs all agencies to prioritize
key RDC-endorsed programs, projects, and activities (PPAs) in their investment programs and
annual budgets, and to provide feedback on the status of these PPAs through their respective
regional offices to the RDCs, thereby reinforcing the role of RDCs in aligning national
budgeting with regional development priorities;

WHEREAS, the National Budget Call for FY 2027, consistent with prior budget preparation
directives, requires ACOs, through their Agency Regional Offices (AROs), and State
Universities and Colleges (SUCs), to provide timely feedback to the RDCs on the inclusion or
exclusion of RDC-endorsed regional PPAs in the budget;

WHEREAS, Section 5.3.3 of the Enhanced RDC VIII Public Investment Management (PIM)
Guidelines enjoins agencies and SUCs to provide updates on the inclusion of endorsed PPAs
in the national budget as part of efforts to strengthen results-based monitoring and
evaluation at the regional level;

WHEREAS, while these policies establish mechanisms for reporting on the inclusion or
exclusion of RDC-endorsed PPAs in the National Expenditure Program (NEP) and the General
Appropriations Act (GAA), the RDC VIII has observed persistent information gaps between
approved appropriations and actual fund releases at the regional level, particularly for funds
managed by ACOs and released to RLAs through sub-allotments;

WHEREAS, these information gaps limit the ability of the RDC Vill to fully track the flow of
funds and assess whether RDC-endorsed PPAs are being funded and implemented as
intended;

WHEREAS, access to detailed sub-allotment reports for RLAs is essential to ensuring and
monitoring consistency between approved appropriations and actual fund releases, enabling
the RDC VIII to effectively track the funding and implementation of its endorsed PPAs,
particularly those under centrally managed items;

WHEREAS, the availability of detailed sub-allotment information will further enhance
transparency, strengthen inter-agency coordination, facilitate accurate monitoring of fund
utilization, and support evidence-based decision-making and policy recommendations of the
RDC VIII;
