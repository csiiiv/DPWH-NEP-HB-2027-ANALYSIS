Dear Administrator Guillen:
Greetings of Peace!

This is to respectfully provide your office with a copy of the Regional Development Council (RDC) IX
Resolution No. 117-D.4, series of 2025, “Endorsing the Updated Regional Development Investment
Program 2023-2028 of the National Irrigation Administration

(NIA) Regional Office IX in the Amount of PHP22.49 billion for Inclusion in the Investment Priorities
and Budget Proposal of the DENR Central Office.”

The said resolution was adopted during the 189th Regular Meeting of RDC IX on December 03, 2025, at
the Grand Astoria Hotel, Zamboanga City.

The priority projects in the Updated RDIP 2023-2028 of NIA IX support the 8-point Socioeconomic
Agenda of the Marcos Jr. Administration as indicated in the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-
2028, the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, the AmBisyon

Natin 2040, the Zamboanga Peninsula Regional Physical Framework Plan (RPFP) 2025-2055, and the
Zamboanga Peninsula Regional Development Plan (RDP) 2023-2028 Midterm Update through its
VIBRANT strategies.

These initiatives are especially vital in addressing the region’s persistent development challenges,
including its high poverty incidence. They are envisioned to significantly advance the region’s vision of
becoming the center of sustainable agri-fishery industries

in the Philippines.

We hope for your favorable consideration of the Updated RDIP 2023-2028 of NIA IX in the Updated
Public Investment Program as input to the FY 2027 Budget Preparation.

Thank you for your continued support in fostering a more economically vibrant Zamboanga Peninsula.

ENDORSING THE UPDATED REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT INVESTMENT
PROGRAM 2023-2028 OF THE NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION
(NIA) REGIONAL OFFICE IX IN THE AMOUNT OF PHP22.49 BILLION FOR

INCLUSION IN THE INVESTMENT PRIORITIES AND BUDGET
PROPOSALS OF THE NIA CENTRAL OFFICE

WHEREAS, the Zamboanga Peninsula (ZamPen) Regional Development Plan 2023-2028
Midterm Update (RDP-MU) was formulated to serve as the blueprint for the
convergence of efforts of all responsible entities, key players and stakeholders
toward the attainment of common goals for the development of the region in
the remaining three years, and it was approved by RDC IX through Resolution
No. 095, series of 2025, on September 4, 2025;

WHEREAS, following the midterm updating of the ZamPen RDP, the ZamPen RDIP 2023-
2028 shall also be updated as an accompanying document of the Plan. The
updated ZamPen RDIP 2023-2028 shall contain the list of programs and
projects (PAPs) to be implemented by the regional line agencies (RLAs), state
universities and colleges (SUCs), special development authorities (SDAs), and
other national government offices that contributes to the achievement of the
socictal goal and targets in the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028,
ZamPen RDP-MU, and aligned with their Results Matrices (RM).;

WHEREAS, | the priority PAPs in the Updated RDIP 2023-2028 of the National Irrigation
Administration (NIA) Regional Office IX are those that support the 8-point
Socioeconomic Agenda of the Marcos Jr. Administration as indicated in the
Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028, the 2030 Agenda for
Sustainable Development, the AmBisyon Natin 2040, the Zamboanga
Peninsula Regional Development Plan (RDP) 2023-2028 Midterm Update,
and the Zamboanga Peninsula Regional Physical Framework Plan (RPFP)
2025-2055, thereby leading towards the attainment of the region’s vision to
become the center of sustainable agri-fishery industries of the Philippines;

WHEREAS, the Updated RDIP 2023-2028 of NIA IX, as reviewed by the Infrastructure
Development Committee (InfraCom) on November 13, 2025, is broken down

Dear Administrator Guillen:
Greetings of Peace!

This is to respectfully provide your office with a copy of the Regional Development Council
(RDC) IX Resolution No. 117-D.4, series of 2025, “Endorsing the Updated Regional
Development Investment Program 2023-2028 of the National Irrigation Administration
(NIA) Regional Office LX in the Amount of PHP22.49 billion for Inclusion in the Investment
Priorities and Budget Proposal of the DENR Central Office.”

The said resolution was adopted during the 189"" Regular Meeting of RDC IX on December 03,
2025, at the Grand Astoria Hotel, Zamboanga City.

The priority projects in the Updated RDIP 2023-2028 of NIA IX support the 8-point
Socioeconomic Agenda of the Marcos Jr. Administration as indicated in the Philippine
Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028, the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, the
AmBisyon Natin 2040, the Zamboanga Peninsula Regional Physical Framework Plan (RPFP)
2025-2055, and the Zamboanga Peninsula Regional Development Plan (RDP) 2023-2028 Midterm
Update through its VIBRANT strategies.

These initiatives are especially vital in addressing the region’s persistent development challenges,
including its high poverty incidence. They are envisioned to significantly advance the region’s
vision of becoming the center of sustainable agri-fishery industries in the Philippines.

We hope for your favorable consideration of the Updated RDIP 2023-2028 of NIA IX in the
Updated Public Investment Program as input to the FY 2027 Budget Preparation.

Thank you for your continued support in fostering a more economically vibrant Zamboanga
Peninsula.

Best regards.

Very truly yours,
