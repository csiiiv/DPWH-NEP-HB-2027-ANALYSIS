Dear Administrator Guillen:
Greetings of Peace!

We are pleased to furnish you with a copy each of the following Regional Development Council (RDC) XII
Resolutions:

a. RDC XII Resolution No. 187, Series of 2026, entitled Endorsing the Top 30 Priority Projects of the
SOCCSKSARGEN Region for the 4th Regional-National Investment Programming (RNIP) Dialogue to
the Concerned Agency Central Offices (ACOs); and

6. RDC XII Resolution No. 188, Series of 2026, entitled Endorsing the Top Three (3) Project Proposals of
the SOCCSKSARGEN Region for Feasibility Study (FS) Preparation under the 4th Regional-National
Investment Programming (RNIP) Dialogue to the Concerned Agency Central Offices (ACOs).

The resolutions were approved during the 94th Regular Meeting of RDC XII held on August 27, 2026, in
Kidapawan City, Province of Cotabato.

In pursuit of the transformation and revitalization of the SOCCSKSARGEN Region toward a more innovative,
safe, and resilient economy, the RDC XII respectfully urges the concerned Agency Central Offices to fully
consider and adopt the Region’s endorsed Top 30 Priority Projects for the 4th RNIP Dialogue, including the
Top Three (3) Project Proposals for Feasibility Study (FS) preparation.

In view thereof, we respectfully seek your favorable consideration and support of these resolutions. We would
likewise appreciate being informed of any action taken by your Office on this matter for the Council's
information and guidance.
