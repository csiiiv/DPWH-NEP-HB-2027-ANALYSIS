RDC XII Resolution No. 130, series of 2026

ENDORSING AND REQUESTING THE NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION
(NIA) CENTRAL OFFICE TO PRIORITIZE AND ALLOCATE FUNDS FOR THE NIA XII
BIG-TICKET PROJECTS FROM 2027 ONWARDS

WHEREAS, Chapter 12 of the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028 and Chapter 13 of the
SOCCSKSARGEN Regional Development Plan (RDP) 2023-2028 Midterm Update
emphasize the need to modernize infrastructure and strengthen water governance
through Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM)as key strategies for ensuring
water security, promoting sustainable agriculture, and enhancing climate resilience;

WHEREAS. | the PDP and RDP recognize that improving agricultural productivity and achieving food
security require the development of resilient, efficient, and sustainable irrigation systems

supported by strategic investments and long-term planning;

WHEREAS, Region XII remains one of the country's major food-producing regions and plays a vital
role in sustaining national food sufficiency. Accordingly, the expansion and
modernization of irrigation infrastructure are essential to increasing cropping intensity,
improving farm productivity, enhancing farmers’ incomes, and reducing vulnerability to

climate-induced water shortages and flooding;

WHEREAS, | the National Irrigation Administration (NIA) Region XII has identified the following
four (4) major irrigation infrastructure projects as priority investment programs:

a. Malitubog-Maridagao River Irrigation System Improvement Project (MRISIP);
b. Lebak Integrated River Irrigation System Project (LIRIP);

¢. Tulunan Small Reservoir Irrigation Project (Tulunan SRIP); and

d. Kabulnan 2 Multipurpose Irrigation and Power Project (K2MIPP);

WHEREAS, the MRISIP is a flagship irrigation initiative aimed at improving irrigation efficiency,
mitigating flooding, and ensuring a reliable water supply to thousands of hectares of
agricultural lands in the Province of Cotabato and adjoining areas, thereby increasing
agricultural productivity and strengthening the resilience of farming communities;

WHEREAS, the LIRIP seeks to harness available water resources in the Municipality of Lebak,
Sultan Kudarat, through the development of an integrated irrigation system that will
expand irrigation service coverage, improve agricultural production, and stimulate
economic growth in the municipality and neighboring areas;

WHEREAS, the Tulunan SRIP is designed to establish a sustainable water storage and irrigation
facility that will provide dependable irrigation services to existing and potential
agricultural areas in the Municipality of Tulunan, Cotabato Province, thereby increasing
farm productivity, improving water-use efficiency, and enhancing the socio-economic
well-being of farmer-beneficiaries;
