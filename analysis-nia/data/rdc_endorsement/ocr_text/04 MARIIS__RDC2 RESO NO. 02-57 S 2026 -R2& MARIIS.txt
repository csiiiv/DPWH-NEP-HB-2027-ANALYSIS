RDC2 Resolution No. 02-11
-Series of 2026-

“ADOPTING THE CAGAYAN VALLEY INTEGRATED STRATEGIC
INFRASTRUCTURE PLAN AS A REGIONAL
INFRASTRUCTURE FRAMEWORK”

WHEREAS, the Cagayan Valley Integrated Strategic Infrastructure Plan (CV
ISIP) as a long-term regional infrastructure framework aims to strengthen
connectivity, improve logistics efficiency, enhance water resource management and
sustainable tourism, promote disaster resilience, and support sustainable economic
transformation in the region and the Northern Luzon;

WHEREAS, the CV ISIP originated from the vision of the current Regional
Development Council (RDC) Chairperson and Cagayan Governor Edgar B. Aglipay,
for a broader regional initiative covering the entire Cagayan Valley Region to
recognize the need for integrated, multi-provincial infrastructure planning and

development;

WHEREAS, the Regional Alignment Consultation Workshop conducted on 22-
23 January 2026, converged various regional stakeholders, local government units,
and relevant national government agencies to ensure regional alignment and
stakeholder participation in the formulation of the CV ISIP;

WHEREAS, subsequent Technical Planning Workshops conducted in the
different provinces further refined the plan and incorporated provincial perspectives
and priorities, namely: Isabela (February 11, 2026), Quirino (February 12, 2026),
Nueva Vizcaya (February 13, 2026), and Batanes (February 25, 2026);

WHEREAS, the CV ISIP, consistent with the development priorities of the
Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028 and the Cagayan Valley Regional
Development Plan (CVRDP) 2023-2028, will further improve connectivity,
concentration, and vulnerability reduction strategies;

WHEREAS, the CV ISIP also complements with the long-term spatial
development directions of the Cagayan Valley Regional Physical Framework Plan
(RPFP) 2025-2055, which promotes balanced regional development, strengthened
connectivity corridors, sustainable resource management, and the development of

strategic growth centers across the region;

WHEREAS, the CV ISIP identifies four strategic banner programs that form the
backbone of the proposed regional infrastructure strategy, namely:

4. Cagayan Valley International Gateway Program (CVIGP),
2. Northeastern Seaboard Development Program (NESDP),
3. Sierra Madre Superhighway and Dam Systems Development Program,

and
4. Cagayan River Waterway Development Program (CRWDP);

WHEREAS, the Project Concept Notes of these banner programs require
further technical development, feasibility study preparation, and potential inclusion in
the national infrastructure pipeline and investment programs of relevant national
government agencies;

WHEREAS, the implementation of the CV ISIP requires coordination among
national government agencies, local government units, regional stakeholders, and
the private sector to ensure strategic alignment, efficient resource mobilization, and
sustainable infrastructure development;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, to
adopt the Cagayan Valley Integrated Strategic Infrastructure Plan as a regional

infrastructure framework for Region 2;

RESOLVED FURTHER, to request the Department of Transportation (DOTh),
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPW), Department of Environment and
Natural Resources (DENR), Department of Tourism (DOT), National Irrigation
Administration (NIA), Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Center, and other concerned
agencies to provide technical and financial support for the preparation of a
comprehensive CV ISIP Master Plan and the conduct of feasibility studies for its

component projects;

RESOLVED FURTHER, to enjoin the provincial, city, and municipal local
government units of the region to support the planning, design, and implementation
of the CV ISIP and to consider integrating its priority programs and projects in their
respective development and land use plans, including but not limited to their
Comprehensive Land Use Plans (CLUPs), Comprehensive Development Plans
(CDPs), Provincial Development and Physical Framework Plans (PDPFPs), and local

investment programs;

RESOLVED FURTHER, to request concerned national government agencies
to align their respective sectoral and development plans, investment program, and
other development initiatives with the CV-ISIP Framework;

RESOLVED FINALLY, that copies of this Resolution be furnished to the DOTr,
DPWH, DENR, DOT, NIA, PPP Center, other concerned national government
agencies and institutions, and all local government units in the region for their

information, support, and appropriate action.

ISIP) as a long-term regional infrastructure framework aims to strengthen
connectivity, improve logistics efficiency, enhance water resource management and
sustainable tourism, promote disaster resilience, and support sustainable economic
transformation in the region and North-Eastern Luzon;

WHEREAS, the Sierra Madre Superhighway and Dam Systems Development
Program as one of the banner programs under the CV ISIP aims to establish an
integrated transport, water resource, energy, tourism and environmental
development corridor along the western side of Sierra Madre foothills in Region 2;

WHEREAS, the Program supports the national and regional development
thrusts and priorities, reflected in the Philippine Development Plan 2023-2028,
National Climate Change Action Plan, Philippine Energy Plan, and Cagayan Valley
Regional Development Plan (RDP) 2023-2028;

WHEREAS, the Program includes the development of a high-standard highway
along the foothills of Sierra Madre corridor to improve connectivity, reduce travel time,
provide redundant access in case of disaster, and enhance logistics and economic
integration by linking key smart growth corridors, urban centers, tourism destinations,
production areas across Cagayan, Isabela, Quirino, and Nueva Vizcaya, thereby
improving accessibility to remote communities and facilitating regional development;

WHEREAS, the Program also integrate multi-purpose dam systems along
major river tributaries of the Sierra Madre to supply household water needs, support
irrigation, mitigate flooding, and generate renewable hydropower;

WHEREAS, the Regional Development Council (RDC) Region 2 recognizes
the need to establish the viability of the program ad its components through
concerned national government agencies and developmen artners;

WHEREAS, Executive Order (EO) No. 82, series of 2025, mandates the
Regional Development Council (RDC) to prioritize, review, and endorse to the
national government the annual budget proposals (BPs) of its member agencies;

WHEREAS, the issuance of National Budget Memorandum (NBM) No. 156 and
Corporate Budget Memorandum (CBM) No. 48 provides the guidelines and
procedures for the formulation of the fiscal year (FY) 2027 BPs of agency regional
offices (AROs), state universities and colleges (SUCs), government-owned and
controlled corporations (GOCCs), and other government instrumentalities:

WHEREAS, the Regional Budget Review Committee (RBRC) comprehensively
reviewed and judiciously deliberated on the FY 2027 BPs during the Budget Review
and Consultation held on 10-13 March 2026, to ensure the proper allocation of the
government's limited resources toward priority investments that will address the
region’s development needs;

WHEREAS, the RBRC considered the alignment of programs, activities, and
projects (PAPs) with national and regional development priorities, as embodied in the
current Administration’s 8-point Socioeconomic Agenda, the updated Philippine
Development Plan 2023-2028, and the Cagayan Valley Regional Development Plan
2023-2028;

WHEREAS, the proposed PAPs were evaluated for their implementation
readiness, taking into account the inclusion in the Public Investment Program 2023-
2028, the Cagayan Valley Regional Development Investment Program 2023-2028,
and required supporting documents;

WHEREAS, the proposed FY 2027 BP considered the agency's absorptive
capacity, as reflected in the budget utilization rate and program/ project
implementation performance to ensure efficient implementation of PAPs and deliver
the intended outputs;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, to
endorse the FY 2027 Budget Proposal of the National Irrigation Adm{njstration “y
