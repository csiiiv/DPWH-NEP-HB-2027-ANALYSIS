2023-2028;

consultations with the Regional Line Agencies (RLAs), State Universities and Colleges (SUCs)
and the Metropolitan Baguio-La Trinidad-ltogon-Sablan-Tuba-Tublay Development Authority
(MBLISTTDA) on February 24-27, 2026 focusing on Tier 2 (new and/or expanded) programs and
projects;

WHEREAS, the NIA-CAR proposes an amount of Fifteen Billion Five Hundred Thirty-One Million
Seven Hundred Ninety-One Thousand Pesos (Php 15,531,791,000.00) for its FY 2027 Budget,
