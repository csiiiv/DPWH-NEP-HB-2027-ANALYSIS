ENDORSING THE FISCAL YEAR (FY) 2027 REGIONAL BUDGET PROPOSAL OF

FOR CONSIDERATION IN ITS FY 2027 BUDGET

WHEREAS, the Regional Development Council of Northern Mindanao (RDC-X), during its
143" Full Council Meeting on 03 December 2025, passed RDC-X Resolution No. 104
{s. 2025), approving and adopting the regional guidelines for the preparation of the
FY 2027 regional budget proposals of agency regional offices (AROs), state
universities and colleges (SUCs), government-owned and controlled corporations
(GOCCs), and other government instrumentalities;

WHEREAS, the RDC-X Infrastructure and Utilities Development Committee (InfraCom)
Secretariat reviewed and validated the budget proposals of the 10 infrastructure and
utilities development-sector AROs! based on the RDC-X-approved regional guidelines
and evaluation criteria as follows:

a) Relevance and responsiveness to the regional development thrusts as indicated in
the Philippine/Regional Development Plans (P/RDP) 2023-2028 Midterm
Updates and the regional priority action areas as identified in the 2024 Northern
Mindanao Full Regional Development Report (RDR) and Interim 2025 RDR;

b) Alignment/consistency with Region X*s Regional Development Investment
Program (RDIP) 2023-2028, 2027 Annual Investment Program (AIP), and PAPs
that advance the MAS ASENSO Northern Mindanao Agenda;

c) Geographic-focus targeting of programs, activities, and projects (PAPs) in focus
barangays under the Convergence Areas for Peace and Development (CAPDev)
program;

d) Compliance with statutory budget allocations for gender and development
(GAD), persons with disabilities (PWD), senior citizens/elderly, children, and
youth, and disaster risk reduction and climate change adaptation (DRR/CCA);

e) Compliance with the 2030 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs); and

f) Consistency with the requirements indicated in the National Budget
Memorandum No. 156 (s. 2026) or the National Budget Call for FY 2027,
particularly:

i. Emphasizing implementation-ready PAPs that demonstrate clear value for
money, defined performance metrics, and sufficient absorptive capacity;
