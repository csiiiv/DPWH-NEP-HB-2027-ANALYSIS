Dear Administrator Guilen:

This is to provide your good office with the Bicol Regional Development Council Resolution
No. 1-06H, s. 2026 endorsing the FY 2027 budget proposal of the NIA Region 5, with the
following priority programs, projects and activities (PPAs):

1. Pili Irrigation Project;

2. Paculago Irrigation Project;

3. Waras Small Reservoir Irrigation Project / Lake Baao Development Project;
4. Bosigon Irrigation Project; and

5. Manga Small Reservoir irrigation Project.

The PPAs contained in the FY 2027 budget proposal will contribute to the attainment of the
desired development outcome of the Philippine Development Plan and the Bicol Regional
Development Plans 2023-2028 on water security, ecological integrity of water systems and
modernizing agriculture. Moreover, these support the attainment of Sustainable Development
Goal 2 on zero hunger; 8 on decent work and economic growth; and 9 on industry, innovation
and infrastructure.

We will appreciate receiving updates on the matter, particularly if the PPAs in the endorsed
budget proposals are funded under the FY 2027 General Appropriations Act.

Thank you for your support in the development of the Bicol region.

RDC 5 Resolution No. 1-06G, s. 2026

ENDORSING THE FY 2027 BUDGET PROPOSAL OF NIA REGION 5 TO
NIA AND DBM CENTRAL OFFICES AND TO CONGRESS FOR
FUNDING UNDER THE FY 2027 GAA

Whereas, Executive Order No. 82, Series of 2025 mandates the Regional
Development Councils (RDCs) to prioritize, review and endorse to the national
government the following: (1) programs, projects, and activities (PPAs) that require
funding support from the national government; (2) annual budget proposals of
government agencies or instrumentalities in the region, such as agency regional
offices, state universities and colleges, and special development authorities; and (3)
national programs and projects that will be implemented in and/or that have an impact
on the region;

Whereas, the Department of Budget and Management issued National Budget
Memorandum No. 156 requiring all concerned agencies to secure RDC endorsement
of their annual budget proposals;

Whereas, the Bicol RDC issued RDC Memorandum Circular No. 05-2025 to
guide the review of the FY 2027 budget proposals to ensure that the
proposed implementation-ready PPAs for inclusion in their budget proposals support
the Philippine Development and Bicol Regional Development Plans 2023-2028;
Sustainable Development Goals and other regional priorities; and are included in the
Regional Development Investment Program (RDIP) 2023-2028;

Whereas, the National Irrigation Administration (NIA) plans, designs,
constructs/improves, operates and administer all national irrigation systems
nationwide;

Whereas, the programs and projects proposed by NIA Region 5 for inclusion
in their FY 2027 Agency Budget Proposal are all in the Regional Development
Investment Program 2023-2028;

Whereas, these programs and projects will contribute to the attainment of the
desired development outcomes of the Philippine and Bicol Regional Development
Plans 2023-2028 on water security, ecological integrity of water systems and
modernizing agriculture;

Whereas, these programs and projects support Sustainable Development
Goals 2 on zero hunger; 8 on decent work and economic growth; and 9 on industry,
innovation and infrastructure;
