RDC VIII Resolution No. 06-H, Series of 2026

ENDORSING THE PRIORITY PROGRAMS, PROJECTS, AND ACTIVITIES (PPAs)
OF THE NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION (NIA) VIII FOR INCLUSION
IN THE FY 2027 NIA BUDGET PROPOSAL

WHEREAS, Department of Budget and Management (DBM) National Budget Memorandum
(NBM) No. 156 or the National Budget Call for FY 2027 directs the preparation of government
agencies’ FY 2027 budget proposals which, among others, prioritizes harmonized strategic
regional plans with national priorities and enhances linkages to achieve equitable regional
investment opportunities and growth;

WHEREAS, Section 2.2 of the same NBM underscores the need to ensure that regional plans
align with national priorities to subsequently promote inclusive growth and equitable regional
investment opportunities, and requests the Department Secretaries or Agency/State University
and College (SUC) heads to ensure that their budget proposals went through the RDC review
process and endorsement as may be applicable;

WHEREAS, Section 4(b) of Executive Order No. 82, Series of 2025, mandates the RDCs to
review and endorse to the national government the respective annual budgets of agency
regional offices (AROs) of the RDC member agencies and projects that support the Regional
Development Plans (RDPs) and/or require funding assistance from the national government,
among others;

WHEREAS, the RDC VIII, through Resolution No. 95, Series of 2025, approved the Council's
enhanced Public Investment Management (PIM) Guidelines, which aims to institutionalize
efficient and harmonized mechanisms for identifying and prioritizing quality PPAs supportive of
the Eastern isayas RDP 2023-2028 regional goals of robust, inclusive, and sustained economic
growth and improved quality of life and well-being of all;

WHEREAS, the enhanced PIM Guidelines prescribed the online multi-dimensional mechanism
in the budget review, prioritization, endorsement, and monitoring processes through the
Regional Inter-agency Task Force on Planning and Budgeting (RIATF-PB), concerned RDC VIII
Sectoral Committees, the RDC VIII Full Council, and external stakeholders;

WHEREAS, the NIA proposed and submitted to this Council 19 FY 2027 Tier 1 and Tier 2 PPAs
amounting to PHP9,542,821,841.00;

WHEREAS, the RIATF-PB reviewed, evaluated, and ranked the Tier 2 new and expanded PPAs
based on the following prioritization criteria: (i) responsiveness to development priorities; (ii)
implementation-readiness; (iii) budget absorptive capacity; (iv) agency ranking; and (v)
investment cost;

WHEREAS, during its first quarter 2026 meeting held on 11 March 2026, the RDC VIII-
Infrastructure Development Committee (IDC), through Resolution No. 03, Series of 2026,
recommended this Council's endorsement of the Tier 1 and Tier 2 priority PPAs of the NIA VIII
for inclusion in the FY 2027 NIA budget;

WHEREAS, as provided in Section 5.3.2.11 of the enhanced RDC VIII PIM Guidelines, all
proposed Tier 2 PPAs shall be evaluated and ranked based on the prioritization criteria, but only
Tier 2 PPAs that are responsive to regional priorities and with complete implementation-
readiness documents will be endorsed by the RDC VIII;

WHEREAS, upon review, this Council finds merit in endorsing only the 15 Tier 1 priority PPAs
of the NIA VIII amounting to PHP8,968,076841.00, considering that their Tier 2 PPAs are not
fully implementation-ready;
