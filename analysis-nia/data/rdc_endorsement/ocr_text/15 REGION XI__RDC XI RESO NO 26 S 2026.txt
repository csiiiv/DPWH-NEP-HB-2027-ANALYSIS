copy of the Regional Development Council (RDC) XI Resolution No. 26, series of 2026, “ENDORSING TO THE
ADMINISTRATOR OF THE NATIONAL IRRIGATION ADMINISTRATION (NIA), THE SECRETARY OF THE
DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT (DBM), AND THE CONGRESSIONAL REPRESENTATIVES OF
DAVAO REGION, THE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AND THE
COMMITTEE ON FINANCE OF THE SENATE OF THE PHILIPPINES, THE FY 2027 BUDGET PROPOSAL OF THE
NIA XI IN THE AMOUNT OF PHP 7,700,874,330.00”.

The NIA XI FY 2027 Budget Proposal was presented by Acting Regional Irrigation Manager Dexter A.
Sablaon, presented before the Infrastructure Development Committee XI during its CY 2026 1st Quarter
Meeting on February 26, 2026 at the DEPDev-RDC XI Center, Davao City.

We would highly appreciate receiving a copy of NIA’s BP Form C with the feedback on the RDC
Inputs/Recommendations, for our reference and monitoring. This is consistent with Section 2.2.3 of the
National Budget Memorandum No. 156 dated January 5, 2026 which states that “the ACOs, through their
AROs, as well as the SUCs, shall provide initial feedback to the concerned RDCs on the disposition of RDC-
endorsed projects. Such feedback should clearly identify the items that were or were not included in their
budget proposals, together with the corresponding justification”.

Thank you and we look forward to NIA and RDC XI’s continued partnership.
