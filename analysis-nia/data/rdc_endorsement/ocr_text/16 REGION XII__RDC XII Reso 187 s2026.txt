DEPARTMENT OF ECONOMY, PLANNING, AND DEVELOPMENT

REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL XII

SOCCSKSARGEN REGION

Excerpts from the Highlights of the 94" Regular Meeting of the Regional Development Council (RDC) XII on
August 27, 2026, at The Capitol Rooftop, 3/F Gov. Rosario P. Diaz Building, Cotabato Provincial Capitol
Complex, Quezon Boulevard, Barangay Amas, Kidapawan City, Province of Cotabato.

RDC XII Resolution No. 187, Series of 2026

ENDORSING THE TOP 30 PRIORITY PROJECTS OF THE SOCCSKSARGEN REGION
FOR THE 4TH REGIONAL-NATIONAL INVESTMENT PROGRAMMING (RNIP)
DIALOGUE TO THE CONCERNED AGENCY CENTRAL OFFICES (ACOs)

WHEREAS, DEPDev Secretary Arsenio M. Balisacan issued a call dated July 20, 2026, directing
the concerned Agency Central Offices (ACOs) and Regional Development Councils
(RDCs) to participate in the 4th Regional-National Investment Programming (RNIP)
Dialogue on September 24, 2026, in Metro Manila, for the alignment of priority
regional projects with national and sectoral priorities and to undertake the necessary
preparatory activities relative thereto;

WHEREAS, __ the RNIP Dialogue aims to strengthen the linkage between the Regional Development
Investment Program (RDIP) and the Public Investment Program (PIP) by ensuring that
regional priorities are effectively integrated into national and sectoral investment
priorities;

WHEREAS, _ the 4th RNIP Dialogue shall build upon the objectives of the first three RNIP Dialogues
and shall cover ten Agency Central Offices (ACOs), including their concerned attached
agencies, namely: the Department of Agriculture (DA), Department of Education
(DepEd), Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), Department of
Health (DOH), Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), Department
of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), Department of Science and Technology
(DOST), Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of
Transportation (DOTr), and the National Irrigation Administration (NIA);

WHEREAS, _ pursuant to the Guidelines on the 4th RNIP Dialogue, all RDCs are tasked to identify
their Top 30 Regional Priority Projects (ARNIPs) for inclusion in the Updated Public
Investment Program (PIP) 2023-2028 and the Three-Year Rolling Infrastructure
Program (TRIP) 2028-2030 as inputs to the FY 2028 national budget preparation
process;

WHEREAS, _ in preparation for the conduct of the 4th RNIP Dialogue and the updating of the PIP
2023-2028, the four RDC XII Sectoral Committees, namely: the Macro-Economy,
Development Administration, and Finance Committee (MEDAFC), Economic
Development Committee (EDC), Infrastructure and Utilities Development Committee
(InfraCom), and Regional Social Development Committee (RSDC), jointly reviewed
and endorsed the updated RDIP 2023-2028 and CY 2028 Annual Investment Program
(AIP) of the SOCCSKSARGEN Region on July 21, 2026;

WHEREAS, _ the RDC XII Executive Committee (ExCom) approved the updated RDIP 2023-2028
and CY 2028 AIP and endorsed the same to the concerned ACOs on July 22, 2026;

WHEREAS, | the updating of the SOCCSKSARGEN RDIP 2023-2028 and its CY 2028 AIP is geared
toward the attainment of AmBisyon Natin 2040, the 2030 Sustainable Development
Goals (SDGs), the Administration's Eight-Point Socioeconomic Agenda, the Philippine
Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028, and the SOCCSKSARGEN Regional
Development Plan (RDP) 2023-2028 Midterm Update;

WHEREAS, _ the RDIP, through its corresponding AIP, serves as the primary reference document in
the preparation of the proposed budgets of regional line agencies (RLAs), state
universities and colleges (SUCs), and other national government instrumentalities

(NGIs);

WHEREAS, _ the updated CY 2028 AIP includes the Top 30 Regional Priority Projects for the 4th
RNIP Dialogue, which are expected to contribute to the attainment of the
SOCCSKSARGEN RDP 2023-2028 Midterm Update goal of achieving socioeconomic
transformation for a prosperous, inclusive, and resilient Region XII by 2028, and
ultimately realizing the long-term vision of a peaceful, safe, resilient, and highly
competitive SOCCSKSARGEN Region as the Agro-Industrial Center and Agri-Eco-
Cultural Tourism Hub of Southern Philippines by 2045;

WHEREAS, | technical meetings between the concerned ACOs and RDCs were conducted from
August 18 to September 4, 2026, to assess, validate, and finalize the Top 30 Regional
Priority Projects prior to the conduct of the RNIP Dialogue proper;

WHEREAS, RDC XII has finalized its Top 30 Regional Priority Projects for the 4th RNIP Dialogue,
attached hereto as Annex C and made an integral part of this Resolution;

NOW, THEREFORE, on motion of Mr. Raymund K. Salangsang, Chairperson of InfraCom XII and
Private Sector Representative (PSR) for General Santos City, duly seconded and
unanimously approved, BE IT RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, That
the RDC XII endorse the Top 30 Priority Projects of the SOCCSKSARGEN Region for
the 4th RNIP Dialogue to the concerned Agency Central Offices (ACOs).

RESOLVED FURTHER, That copies of this Resolution, together with its attachment, be furnished to
the Central and Region XII Offices of DA, DENR, DOH, DILG, including BFP, BJMP,
and PNP, DPWH, DOST, DSWD, DOTr, and NIA, and the RNIP Secretariat for
information, consideration, and appropriate action.

DONE this 27th day of August 2026 in Kidapawan City, Province of Cotabato, Philippines.
