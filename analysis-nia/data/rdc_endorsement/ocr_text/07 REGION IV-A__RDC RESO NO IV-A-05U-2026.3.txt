WHEREAS, Section 3.6 of the implementing Guidelines of Executive Order No. 82, s. 2025
mandates the Regional Development Council (RDC) to prioritize, review, and,
as applicable, endorse to the national government the annual budgets of
government agencies or instrumentalities in the region, such as but not limited
to, agency regional offices (AROs), state universities and colleges (SUCs),
and special development authorities;

WHEREAS, the CALABARZON RDC issued the Guidelines for Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 RDC
Budget Review on December 4, 2025, and the Department of Budget and
Management (DBM) issued the National Budget Memorandum No. 156 on
January 5, 2026, to guide the government agencies in the preparation of their

budget proposals;

WHEREAS, programs, activities, and projects (PAPs) in the FY 2027 budget proposal of
the National Irrigation Administration (NIA) Region lV-A were reviewed during
the Technical Budget Review on January 27, 2026, considering the priority
PAPs from the 2025 Regional-National Investment Program Dialogue and the
2025 CALABARZON Preliminary Regional Development Report;

WHEREAS, NIA Region IV-A, an attached government-owned and controlled corporation
of the Department of Agriculture, submitted its FY 2027 Budget Proposal, as
