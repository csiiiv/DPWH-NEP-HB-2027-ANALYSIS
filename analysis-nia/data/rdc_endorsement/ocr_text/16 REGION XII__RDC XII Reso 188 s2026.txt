DEPARTMENT OF ECONOMY, PLANNING, AND DEVELOPMENT

REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL XII

SOCCSKSARGEN REGION

Excerpts from the Highlights of the 94" Regular Meeting of the Regional Development Council (RDC) XII on
August 27, 2026, at The Capitol Rooftop, 3/F Gov. Rosario P. Diaz Building, Cotabato Provincial Capitol
Complex, Quezon Boulevard, Barangay Amas, Kidapawan City, Province of Cotabato.

RDC XII Resolution No. 188, Series of 2026

ENDORSING THE TOP THREE (3) PROJECT PROPOSALS OF THE SOCCSKSARGEN
REGION FOR FEASIBILITY STUDY (FS) PREPARATION UNDER THE 4TH REGIONAL-
NATIONAL INVESTMENT PROGRAMMING (RNIP) DIALOGUE TO THE CONCERNED

AGENCY CENTRAL OFFICES (ACOs)

WHEREAS, _DEPDev Secretary Arsenio M. Balisacan, through a Call dated July 20, 2026, directed
the concerned Agency Central Offices (ACOs) and Regional Development Councils
(RDCs) to participate in the 4th Regional-National Investment Programming (RNIP)
Dialogue on September 24, 2026, in Metro Manila to align priority regional projects
with national and sectoral priorities and undertake the necessary preparatory activities
relative thereto;

WHEREAS, _ the RNIP Dialogue aims to strengthen the linkage between the Regional Development
Investment Program (RDIP) and the Public Investment Program (PIP) by ensuring that
regional development priorities are adequately considered in national and sectoral
investment programming;

WHEREAS, | the 4th RNIP Dialogue shall build on the gains and objectives of the first three RNIP
Dialogues and shall cover ten (10) Agency Central Offices, including their concerned
attached agencies, namely: DA, DepEd, DENR, DOH, DILG, DPWH, DOST, DSWD,
DOTr, and NIA;

WHEREAS, _ pursuant to the Guidelines on the Conduct of the 4th RNIP Dialogue, the RDCs are
tasked to identify their top three (3) priority project proposals requiring Feasibility
Study (FS) preparation and funding support;

WHEREAS, _ in preparation for the conduct of the 4th RNIP Dialogue and the updating of the PIP
2023-2028, the four RDC XII Sectoral Committees, namely the MEDAFC, EDC,
InfraCom, and RSDC, simultaneously reviewed and endorsed the updated
SOCCSKSARGEN Regional Development Investment Program (RDIP) 2023-2028
and CY 2028 Annual Investment Program (AIP) on July 21, 2026;

WHEREAS, | the RDC XII Executive Committee (ExCom), on July 22, 2026, approved the updated
RDIP 2023-2028/CY 2028 AIP and endorsed the same to the concerned Agency Central
Offices for consideration in the updating of the PIP 2023-2028;

WHEREAS, | the updating of the SOCCSKSARGEN RDIP 2023-2028 and its CY 2028 AIP is
anchored on the attainment of AmBisyon Natin 2040, the 2030 Sustainable
Development Goals (SDGs), the Eight-Point Socioeconomic Agenda of the Marcos
Administration, the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028, and the
SOCCSKSARGEN Regional Development Plan (RDP) 2023-2028 Midterm Update;

WHEREAS, | the RDIP, through its corresponding AIP, serves as the primary reference document in
the preparation of the proposed budgets of Regional Line Agencies (RLAs), State
Universities and Colleges (SUCs), and other National Government Instrumentalities

(NGIs);

WHEREAS, the Council identified three (3) priority project proposals requiring funding support
from the concerned Agency Central Offices for the conduct of Feasibility Studies;

WHEREAS, | technical meetings between the concerned Agency Central Offices and Regional
Development Councils were conducted from August 18 to September 4, 2026, to assess,
validate, and finalize the top three (3) project proposals for Feasibility Study
preparation prior to the RNIP Dialogue proper;

WHEREAS, RDC XII has finalized its top three (3) project proposals for Feasibility Study
preparation under the 4"" RNIP Dialogue, as reflected in Annex E attached hereto;

NOW, THEREFORE, on motion of Engr. Arturo D. Zambrano, Private Sector Representative (PSR)
for the Province of Sultan Kudarat and Tacurong City, duly seconded and unanimously
approved, BE IT RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, That the RDC XII
endorse the Top Three (3) Project Proposals of the SOCCSKSARGEN Region for
Feasibility Study Preparation under the 4th RNIP Dialogue to the concerned Agency
Central Offices for consideration and appropriate funding support.

RESOLVED FURTHER, That copies of this Resolution, together with its attachment, be furnished to
the Central and Region XII Offices of the National Irrigation Administration (NIA) and
the Department of Public Works and Highways (DP WH), and the RNIP Secretariat for
their information, consideration, and appropriate action.

DONE this 27th day of August 2026 in Kidapawan City, Province of Cotabato, Philippines.
