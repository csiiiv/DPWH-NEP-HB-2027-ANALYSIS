ENDORSING THE PROPOSED FY 2027 BUDGET OF THE NATIONAL IRRIGATION
ADMINISTRATION (NIA) REGIONAL OFFICE VI FOR
INCLUSION IN NIA CENTRAL OFFICE’S FY 2027 BUDGET PROPOSAL

WHEREAS, Section 4 of Executive Order No. 82, s. 2025, mandates the RDC to
review and endorse to the national government the annual budget of regional offices;

WHEREAS, Section 2.2.3 of the Department of Budget and Management's National
Budget Memorandum No. 156 dated January 5, 2026 (National Budget Call for FY 2027),
requires Heads of Agencies to ensure that their budget proposals have gone through the RDC
review and endorsement process, as may be applicable;

WHEREAS, the RDC VI, through its Infrastructure Development Committee (IDC),
together with select RDC Vi members and the focal staff of Congressional representatives,
reviewed and deliberated the FY 2027 NIA Regional Office VI on February 26, 2026;

WHEREAS, proposed programs and projects listed in NIA Region VI’s budget proposal
are aligned with the development priorities and strategies of the following:

Midterm Update of the Western Chapters 12 - Expand and Upgrade Infrastructure
Visayas Regional Development
Plan 2023-2028

Marcos Administration's 8 Point- Agenda 4 - Create More Jobs
Socioeconomic Agenda
Sustainable Development Goals _ Goals 6 — Clean Water & Sanitation

WHEREAS, the details of NIA Region VI’s budget proposal for FY2027 is attached as
Annex A;

WHEREAS, the Tier 1 and Tier 2 Budget Summary (Budget Preparation Form C),
including the RDC VI inputs and recommendations, is attached as Annex B;

WHEREAS, NIA Region VI's List of Proposed CY2027 Infrastructure Projects, is
attached as Annex C;

WHEREAS, the Infrastructure Development Committee favorably recommends the
endorsement of the FY2027 budget proposal of NIA Region VI;

NOW, THEREFORE, on motion of IDC Chairperson, Engr. Nilo J. Jardeleza of Rotary
Club of Iloilo South, which was severally seconded, BE IT RESOLVED, as it is HEREBY
RESOLVED, to endorse the proposed FY 2027 budget of NIA Regional Office VI amounting
to PhP 15,571.169 million, for inclusion in NIA Central Office’s FY2027 Budget Proposal;

at Department of Economy, Planning, and Development Regional Office VI
