RDC Vill Resolution No. 9, Series of 2026

ENJOINING THE AGENCY CENTRAL OFFICES (ACOs) TO PROVIDE TIMELY AND SUFFICIENT
FEEDBACK TO THE REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT COUNCILS (RDCs) ON THE STATUS OF INCLUSION
OF RDC-ENDORSED PROGRAMS, PROJECTS, AND ACTIVITIES (PPAs) IN THE
ACO BUDGET, NATIONAL EXPENDITURE PROGRAM (NEP), AND GENERAL
APPROPRIATIONS ACT (GAA), INCLUDING THE REASONS THEREFOR

WHEREAS, Executive Order (EO) No. 82, Series of 2025, mandates the RDCs to review, prioritize, and
endorse priority PPAs consistent with regional development priorities for national government
support and ensure the effective linkage between regional planning, investment programming, and
budgeting;

WHEREAS, the same EO and its Implementing Guidelines further require ACOs to prioritize RDC-
endorsed PPAs in their investment programs and annual budgets, and to report to the RDCs on the
actions taken on these regional priorities;

WHEREAS, National Budget Memorandum (NBM) No. 156 or the National Budget Call for FY 2027
reinforces the role of the RDCs in the national budgeting process and highlights the agency-RDC
feedback mechanism, including the requirement for ACOs and state universities and colleges (SUCs)
to provide written feedback on the status of RDC-endorsed PPAs;

WHEREAS, NBM 156 further requires ACOs and SUCs to justify the inclusion or non-inclusion of their
RDC-endorsed PPAs through proper documentation and the submission of Budget Preparation (BP)
Form C (Summary of RDC Inputs and Recommendations on Agency Programs and Projects) to the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM);

WHEREAS, the enhanced RDC VIII Public Investment Management (PIM) Guidelines provides the
mechanisms for the systematic review, prioritization, and monitoring of RDC Vill-endorsed PPAs
throughout the budget cycle, and require agencies to provide updates on their inclusion in the NEP
and GAA, including the reasons for non-inclusion;

WHEREAS, during its first quarter 2026 meeting, the RDC VIII-Environment Committee (EnC) noted
that all FY 2026 Tier 2 PPAs in the environment sector, as endorsed by the Council, were not included
in either the NEP or the GAA, constituting a critical disconnect that constrains the implementation
of priority interventions on climate change and disaster resilience;

WHEREAS, the RDC Vill-Social Development Committee (SDC) likewise recognized the limited
availability of timely updates from concerned agencies, noting that the lack of transparent feedback
impedes effective tracking of proposals and the conduct of subsequent regional budget reviews;

WHEREAS, during its first quarter 2026 meetings, the RDC VIII-EnC passed Resolution No. 3, Series
of 2026, and the RDC VIII-SDC likewise passed Resolution No. 3, Series of 2026, Recommending to
the RDC VIII to Enjoin the Agency Central Offices (ACOs) to Provide Timely and Sufficient Feedback to

the RDCs on the Status of Inclusion of RDC-Endorsed Program, Projects, and Activities (PPAs) in the
ACO Budget, National Expenditure Program, and General Appropriations Act, Including the Reasons

WHEREAS, in view of the foregoing, the Council recognizes the need to institutionalize a more
robust, transparent, and data-driven feedback mechanism that ensures accountability and provides
clear and actionable justifications for the inclusion, partial inclusion, or exclusion of RDC-endorsed
PPAs, thereby enabling better alignment of future regional investment programming and budgeting
with national priorities, as outlined in the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028 and the
Eastern Visayas Regional Development Plan (EV RDP) 2023-2028.

NOW, THEREFORE, upon the motion of RDC VIII-SDC Co-Chairperson and Private Sector
Representative (PSR) for Labor, Bellsedaize C. Acaso, duly seconded by the RDC VIII-PSR for
Infrastructure, Angelito C. Cafiete, BE IT RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, to enjoin the ACOs
to provide timely and sufficient feedback to the RDCs on the status of inclusion of RDC-endorsed
PPAs in the ACO budget, NEP, and GAA, including the reasons therefor.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the ACOs shall clearly indicate the technical, fiscal, or policy-based reasons
for the non-inclusion, partial inclusion, or modification of such PPAs, and ensure compliance with
the existing budget call through the timely accomplishment and submission of BP Form C.

RESOLVED FINALLY, that copies of this Resolution be transmitted to all concerned ACOs for their
information and appropriate action, with copies furnished to the respective regional line agencies in
Eastern Visayas.

UNANIMOUSLY APPROVED this 31° day of March, Two Thousand and Twenty-Six, during the First
Quarter 2026 RDC VIII Full Council Meeting conducted at the Felipina Hall, DEPDev Regional Office
VII, Palo, Leyte, and via Zoom.
